🕹️ HOW TO PLAY

⌨️ PC Controls

AD Move left / right
W Jump  |  W W Flip Jump (double-tap)!
J Punch  |  K Kick  |  L Special
F Block (hold)  |  SPC Dodge
T Taunt (+SP & intimidate!)
ESC Quit to menu

📱 Mobile

Use the on-screen D-pad and action buttons.
SP button pulses when your special is ready!

⚡ Combat Tips

🔥 Fill your SP bar to unleash your special move.
🛡️ Block reduces damage to ~12% — use it wisely.
💨 Dodge costs 22 SP but grants full invincibility.
🎯 Taunting builds SP and intimidates enemies.
⚠️ RAGE MODE activates below 25% HP — stay sharp!
